# Code of Conduct

This project follows the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
It applies in the project's issues, pull requests, discussions and any other place where the
project is represented.

## In short

- Be welcoming and patient. People arrive with different experience, languages and backgrounds.
- Be respectful when you disagree. Criticise the work, not the person.
- Give and accept feedback gracefully.
- No harassment, insults, sexualised language or imagery, or publishing someone's private
  information.

The museum is about stories that many people love and disagree about. Arguments over which
series or character is better do not belong in the issue tracker.

## Reporting

If you see or experience behaviour that breaks this code, report it privately to the project
maintainers. Their contact details are on the GitHub profile of the repository's owner. Reports
are kept confidential.

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, issues and other contributions that do
not follow this code, and may ban a contributor temporarily or permanently for behaviour they
consider inappropriate, threatening or harmful.
